# Vigil — walkthrough voiceover (~90 seconds)

Record your screen at vigil-gules.vercel.app. Narrate cold and calm, no hype.
Read the real numbers that show on your own capital-preserved card.

---

**[0:00–0:10] Cold open on the Market Clock**
On screen: the hero, NYSE ASLEEP, the unprotected countdown ticking, SOLANA AWAKE.
VO: "Tokenized stocks trade around the clock on Solana. The New York Stock Exchange does not. Right now it is dark, and it stays dark for the next two and a half days."

**[0:10–0:20] The insight**
On screen: slow zoom on the cold line.
VO: "Every stop-loss on a real share dies at the closing bell. So here is the problem no brokerage will solve. A stop-loss that only works while the market is open is not a stop-loss. It is a suggestion."

**[0:20–0:30] Name it**
On screen: scroll to Holdings and the live watchlist, prices ticking.
VO: "Vigil is the watch that never closes. A non-custodial guard for tokenized stocks. It reads the live Pyth feed and acts on your rules while your broker sleeps."

**[0:30–0:42] Night Watch**
On screen: flip the Night Watch toggle. It reads "Night Watch active. Rules are live." with the green glow.
VO: "Turn on Night Watch and your rules stand guard only during the hours you are unprotected. Wall Street is dark, so Vigil is live."

**[0:42–0:52] Arm a rule**
On screen: Autopilot, pick Stop loss, click "Arm stop loss on NVDAx." The armed pill appears.
VO: "Set a floor on NVIDIA. If it breaks, sell to stablecoin. One rule, armed in seconds."

**[0:52–1:12] The drill (the money shot)**
On screen: Fire drill, click Crash minus 25 percent. The price flashes red, the guard fires, the card lands.
VO: "Now watch what happens when the market moves against you at 3am on a Sunday. I inject a 25 percent crash. The floor breaks. Vigil fires instantly."
(pause, let the card land)
VO: "Held to the low you were down [read the red number]. Vigil sold in time and preserved [read the green number]. NYSE was closed. A broker stop could not have done this."

**[1:12–1:26] On-chain proof**
On screen: the Resting on Solana panel and the verifier. Paste a wallet address, open an order on Solscan.
VO: "This is not a mockup. Take-profit and buy-the-dip rest on Solana as real Jupiter orders, filled by the keeper around the clock with your tab closed. Paste any wallet and verify the orders yourself on Solscan. Vigil signs nothing without you and never holds your keys."

**[1:26–1:36] Close**
On screen: back to the two clocks, the Protected counter in the header.
VO: "The stock market is open for building. Vigil makes owning it safer than a brokerage, because it never sleeps. Your brokerage closes. Your money should not."

## Recording tips
- Use a wide browser window so the top-bar clock (NYSE / SOLANA / PROTECTED) is visible.
- Do a dry run of the drill first so the numbers are fresh; use "reset demo book" between takes.
- Keep it under 100 seconds. Judges watch a lot of these. Lead with the clock, close on the card.
- If you place a real $5 order later, re-record the on-chain beat showing your own order on Solscan.
