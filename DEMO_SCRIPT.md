# Vigil — the winning demo (shot-by-shot)

Target length: 90 seconds. Judges watch dozens of these. Short, calm, and real beats long and hyped every time.

## Before you hit record (2 minutes of prep)

1. **Timing.** Record when the NYSE is DARK, so the clock tells the story. Any evening after ~9:00 PM your time, or anytime on a weekend. Confirm the top bar says **NYSE ASLEEP** and the dial says **NYSE DARK**. (A weekend is best: the countdown reads days, not hours.)
2. **Clean the browser.** Full-screen the window at a wide size. Close other tabs. Hide the bookmarks bar. One clean tab on `vigil-gules.vercel.app`.
3. **Load the demo book fresh.** Top right, click **Connect / Demo → Explore with demo wallet**. Then in the **Fire drill** panel (right column), click **reset demo book & clear rules** once so the numbers are clean.
4. **Recorder.** QuickTime (Mac: File → New Screen Recording), Loom, or OBS. Record the browser window only. Do one silent dry run so your clicks are smooth.
5. **Voice.** Calm and slow. Let the screen breathe. No background music, or very quiet. You are an operator showing a tool, not a salesman.
6. **Have these ready** to paste/open for the closer:
   - Order on-chain: `https://explorer.solana.com/address/3bD9kJGgxGHmiPNSuK69UDZcGFwuucgTKwrfmmGj6Q9m`
   - Wallet to paste in the verifier: `GKJQFHmyuSxS55BhTVCMBabAYtVFGT3Fg5toifuSeiNC`

## The script

Format: **[DO]** = what to click/show · **"SAY"** = word for word.

### 1 · Cold open (0:00–0:12)
**[DO]** Start on the top of the page: the hero and the dial. Do not move for a beat.
**"SAY:"** "Right now, the New York Stock Exchange is closed. It is closed about eighty percent of the week. But these are tokenized stocks on Solana, and they never stop trading. So when bad news breaks at 3am on a Sunday, your broker is asleep, your stop-loss is asleep, and your money is exposed."

### 2 · The line (0:12–0:20)
**[DO]** Let the cold line sit on screen: "A stop-loss that only works while the market is open is not a stop-loss. It is a suggestion."
**"SAY:"** "A stop-loss that only works while the market is open is not a stop-loss. It is a suggestion. Vigil fixes that."

### 3 · It is live and real (0:20–0:32)
**[DO]** Point to the dial, then scroll down slowly to the **Tokenized stocks** watchlist with the ticking prices.
**"SAY:"** "Vigil is a twenty-four-seven guard for tokenized stocks. Everything here is live. The clock is Pyth market data. The prices are live from Jupiter. Every one of these trades around the clock while Wall Street sleeps."

### 4 · Night Watch (0:32–0:42)
**[DO]** In the right column, flip the **Night Watch** toggle on. It turns green and reads "Night Watch active. Rules are live."
**"SAY:"** "I turn on Night Watch. Now my rules stand guard only during the hours my broker cannot. It reads active, because right now, Wall Street is dark."

### 5 · Arm one rule (0:42–0:52)
**[DO]** In the **Autopilot** panel, **Stop loss** is already selected. Click the green **Arm stop loss on NVDAx** button. It appears in the armed list below.
**"SAY:"** "I set one rule on NVIDIA. If it falls to my floor, sell. Armed in two seconds. Vigil now reads the live feed every second so I do not have to."

### 6 · The crash (0:52–1:10) — the peak
**[DO]** In the **Fire drill** panel, click **Crash −25%**. Watch the price flash red, the guard fire, and the green **Capital preserved** card land.

### 7 · The proof (1:10–1:28) — the mic drop

It is resting on Solana right now, waiting to buy the dip, and Jupiter's keeper will fill it whether my laptop is open or closed. Do not take my word for it. Here it is on-chain. Paste the wallet. Anyone can verify it. No connection needed."

**[DO]** Scroll back to the top: the two clocks, NYSE dark, Solana awake.
**"SAY:"** "Wall Street closes. Your xStocks do not. Vigil is the watch that never closes."
*(hold on the screen for 2 seconds, then stop recording)*

## Why this beats the clean videos

- **You open on the problem, not the product.** Judges feel the pain in the first ten seconds.
- **You prove it is live** (Pyth, Jupiter) instead of claiming it.
- **You have one emotional peak** (the preserved number) and one undeniable fact (a real order on-chain). Most demos have neither.
- **You end on a line people repeat.** "Wall Street closes. Your xStocks don't."
- **You are honest.** You show a real order and let them verify it. That builds more trust than any amount of polish.
